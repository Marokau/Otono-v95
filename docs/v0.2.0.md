# v0.2.0 Kerning City Party Quest and bot crew

- Play Kerning City Party Quest with a human party or lead a dedicated bot crew recruited through Lakelis. Use `@wherelevel` for the summary and lobby teleport.
- The crew helps collect coupons, solve the rope and platform puzzles, and defeat King Slime. The final reward box can be selected in the client.
- The bundled client DLL restores the damage numbers and monster flinch for nearby Magic Claw hits from the crew mage. The in-game audit confirmed both close and ranged casts.
- Existing installs can use **Repair Setup** to install the updated client DLL. First-time installs receive it through **Initial Setup**.
