# v0.3.0 Ludibrium Party Quest and bot crew

## Ludibrium Party Quest

Open `@wherelevel`, choose **Party Quests → Ludibrium PQ**, and travel to Eos Tower 101st Floor. Speak to the Red Sign to enter with your own party or assemble three level-35 crew members. Entry is open to parties of 3–6 players at levels 30–200. The time limit is 60 minutes.

The crew helps clear the rats, collect passes, fight through the dark rooms, navigate the numbered boxes, and solve the equation. In the final room, defeat Alishar to unlock the treasure boxes. His summons can still be alive when you claim your reward.

## Launcher updates

- **Updating from v0.2.1:** Close the game, server and launcher. Extract **Otono-v95-Updater.zip** into your existing Otono folder, then run `launcher/Check-for-Updates.cmd`. Confirm the update and reopen `Otono.cmd`. Your SQLite characters, game files and `config.yaml` stay in place.
- **Fresh install:** Download the source ZIP and open `Otono.cmd` to run **Initial Setup**.
- Starting with v0.3.0, the launcher includes **Check for Updates**.
