# Launcher wallpaper

background.jpg is the user-selected MapleStory wallpaper from Nexon's website:
https://nxcache.nexon.net/umbraco/5190/maplestory_wallpaper_01_1600x1200.jpg

Downloaded November 14, 2026. The original image and its embedded copyright notice are retained. Artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; this is not original Otono artwork.

Replace background.jpg with another local JPG to change the wallpaper. The launcher loads it at startup. If missing or unreadable, it falls back to the classic theme. The Classic look / Wallpaper look link switches appearance and remembers the choice locally under ignored launcher/bin/theme.txt. No download occurs during ordinary launcher startup.
